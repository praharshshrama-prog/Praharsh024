Our Story

Rooted in
heritage.

PRAHARSH — meaning the first light of dawn — was born from a belief that clothing is an art form. We blend India's rich textile traditions with modern, refined silhouettes to create garments that feel as extraordinary as they look.

Every stitch carries the legacy of our artisans. Every fabric is chosen with care. Every design is made to last a lifetime.
